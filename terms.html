Your account

You sign in with Apple or Google. You are responsible for keeping access to that account secure. You must be at least 13 years old to use Seicho.

Your entries are yours

You keep all rights to everything you write. We claim no ownership over your entries and do not publish, share, or sell them. We do not use them to train any model.

To generate feedback, the text of an entry is sent to Anthropic's Claude API and processed there. This is the core function of the app and cannot be separated from it. See the privacy policy for details of what is sent and to whom.

Seicho does not rewrite, edit, or replace your entries. The one modification the app makes is adding punctuation and capitalization to dictated text before you save it, and it verifies that every word is unchanged before showing you the result.

Subscriptions

Seicho is free to use. The free plan includes 10 feedback reports per calendar month, reset on the 1st. Writing and reading entries is never limited — only the generation of new feedback is.

Seicho Premium removes that limit and is offered as an auto-renewing subscription, available monthly or annually. Specific prices are shown in the app before purchase, in your local currency.

  • Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID account settings after purchase.

Purchases are handled by Apple, not by us. Refunds are at Apple's discretion and are requested through Apple, not through this app.
