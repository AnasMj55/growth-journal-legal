Growth Journal — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Growth Journal is a journaling app that gives you feedback on your writing craft. This policy explains what the app collects, where it goes, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

Growth Journal is made and operated by Anas Majumder, an individual developer ("we", "I"). For anything in this policy, contact anas.majumder@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your journal entries are stored in your own private area of our database and are not readable by other users.
  • Entry text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API so the app can generate writing feedback. This is the core function of the app and cannot be turned off separately.
  • Dictated audio is transcribed by your device's operating system. We never receive or store the audio — only the resulting text.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDK, and no third-party marketing in this app. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

What we collect

Account information

Signing in uses Google Sign-In. From your Google account we receive and store your email address, and Google's authentication service additionally holds your display name, profile photo URL, and a Google account identifier. We do not receive your Google password and have no access to anything else in your Google account.

Your journal entries

The text of every entry you save, whether typed or dictated, along with the date and time it was created and whether it came from typing or dictation.

Feedback and progress data

Data the app derives from your entries: rubric scores across four writing dimensions (specificity, structure, voice, clarity), the inline comments generated for each entry, comparison notes when you compare two entries, and running aggregates used to detect writing habits you have grown out of.

Voice recordings

When you dictate an entry, your device's built-in speech recognition captures audio and converts it to text. On iOS this is Apple's speech recognition service and on Android it is Google's; depending on your device and settings, that audio may be transmitted to and processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. Growth Journal never receives, transmits, or stores the audio itself. Only the transcribed text reaches the app, and only if you then save the entry.

What we do not collect

We do not use any analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting SDK. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health data, or advertising identifiers. We do not build behavioural profiles and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How long we keep it

Journal entries and their feedback are kept until you delete them or until your account is deleted. Deleting an entry in the app removes it permanently and it cannot be recovered. Account records are deleted when you request account deletion, as described below.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete an entry. Open the entry and delete it. This is immediate and irreversible.
  • Delete your account and everything in it. Open Settings in the app and choose Delete account. This immediately and permanently erases your account, all of your entries, and all derived feedback and progress data. It cannot be undone. If you cannot access the app, email anas.majumder@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we will delete it for you within 30 days.
  • Get a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it. Email the same address.
  • Revoke access. You can disconnect Growth Journal from your Google account at any time through your Google account's security settings.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, or to complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Your entries are stored in a per-user area of the database enforced by server-side security rules, so one signed-in user cannot read another's entries. Data is encrypted in transit, and encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Growth Journal is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Beta testing

While the app is distributed for testing through TestFlight or Google Play testing, it is pre-release software. It may contain bugs, and features may change or be removed. We may contact testers at the email address associated with their account about the test itself.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change is significant, we will notify you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
